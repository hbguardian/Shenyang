🕵️ How to check who a factory really makes for
  1. Ask: 你们给哪些美国品牌做过？ (“Which US brands do you make for?”) Write down the names.
  2. Send the names to Harris. He searches each brand on ImportYeti.com, which shows US customs records: which Chinese factory shipped to that brand.
  3. Factory name on the brand's shipments = real. Not there = maybe a trading company or a claim.
  4. For electronics: the FCC ID on the brand's product shows who made it. Look it up on fccid.io.
  5. Ask for 2 customers you can contact. A good factory says yes.

Check it yourself

  1. Company license: search the name or credit code on gsxt.gov.cn. It should say 在营 ("active") and list this product.
  2. FDA number: look it up on the FDA 510(k) database. Many pages say "FDA" with no real clearance.
  3. UL / ETL number: look it up in the UL Product iQ or Intertek directory.
  4. Inspection before shipping: hire QIMA or SGS to check the goods at the factory.
  5. Shipping: a freight forwarder in China, a licensed customs broker in New Jersey. Dalian to New York is about 30–40 days by sea.
  6. Tariff: have the broker confirm the duty for each product before ordering. Most Chinese goods pay 37.5% extra (25% from 2018 plus 12.5% since July 24, 2026). A separate medical-device tariff review is still open.
